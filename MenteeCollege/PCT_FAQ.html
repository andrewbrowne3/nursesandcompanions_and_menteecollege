Patient Care Technician (PCT) Program FAQ

PCT Program Duration

  • Duration: 12 weeks
  • Days: Every Wednesday
  • Time: 9:30 AM to 3 PM

Application & Fees

  • Application Fee: $35.00 (non-refundable)
  • Initial Payment: $200.00 + $35.00 application fee due at registration

PCT Requirements

  • Valid CNA license required
  • 2 forms of ID
  • High school/GED diploma

Tuition & Financial Info

  • Regular Price: $4,191.78
  • Special Price: $2,100.00
  • Payment Plan available upon request

National License Exam Information

  • Exam Fee: Included in tuition
  • Exam consists of 120 questions
  • Three opportunities to pass the exam within one year
  • Retake Fee: $190.00
  • National Exam is taken online at the college

Thursday - 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM
Friday - 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM
