Medication Aide Certification Program FAQ - Georgia

Advance Your CNA Career!

Add medication administration to your skillset and increase your earning potential.

Program Overview

The Medication Aide program is designed for Certified Nursing Assistants (CNAs) who want to expand their scope of practice. In just 40 hours, you'll learn safe medication administration techniques and earn your state certification.

  • Duration: 40 Hours
  • Prerequisite: Valid CNA License Required
  • Certification: Georgia State Medication Aide Certification
  • Format: Classroom instruction with hands-on practice

What You'll Learn

  • Medication administration routes (oral, topical, eye/ear drops, inhalers)
  • The "Six Rights" of medication administration
  • Reading and interpreting medication orders
  • Medication documentation and record keeping
  • Common medications and their effects
  • Recognizing adverse reactions and side effects
  • Proper medication storage and handling
  • Legal and ethical responsibilities
  • Infection control during medication administration

Prerequisites

  • Valid CNA License: Must have current Georgia CNA certification
  • Two forms of ID (Photo ID + SS Card)
  • High school diploma or GED

Don't have your CNA yet? Check out our 6-week CNA program first!

Tuition & Fees

  • Tuition: $650.00
  • Application Fee: $35.00 (non-refundable)
  • Payment Plan Available Upon Request
  • State certification exam fee included

Career Opportunities

As a Certified Medication Aide, you can work in various healthcare settings:

  • Nursing homes and long-term care facilities
  • Assisted living facilities
  • Rehabilitation centers
  • Group homes
  • Home health agencies

Salary Boost: Medication Aides typically earn $2-5 more per hour than CNAs without this certification!

Why Add Medication Aide to Your CNA?

  • Higher earning potential
  • More job opportunities
  • Expanded scope of practice
  • Greater job security
  • Path to nursing career
  • Valuable skills for patient care
