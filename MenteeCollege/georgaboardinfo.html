Mentee College Educational Programs

Mentee College, authorized by the State of Georgia Nonpublic Postsecondary Education Commission, offers a range of certificate programs and associate degrees in the healthcare field.

  • Certificate in Patient Care Technician
  • Certificate in EKG Technician
  • Certificate in Phlebotomy Technician
  • Certificate in Medical Assistant
  • Certificate in Medical Billing and Coding
  • Associate of Applied Science in Medical Assistant
  • Associate of Science Diagnostic Medical Sonography

The Certified Nurse Assistant program is authorized by the Georgia Medical Care Foundation.

The Practical Nurse program is approved by the Georgia Board of Nursing.
