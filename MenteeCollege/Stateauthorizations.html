Welcome to Mentee College


Mentee College is authorized by the State of Georgia Nonpublic Postsecondary Education Commission to provide education in several fields. Here are the programs we currently offer:

  • Certificate in Medical Assistant

The Certified Nurse Assistant program is authorized by the Georgia Medical Care Foundation.

The Practical Nurse program is approved by the Georgia Board of Nursing.

Each program is designed to provide students with the necessary skills and knowledge to excel in the healthcare field.
