Program Overview

Our American Heart Association (AHA) BLS Provider course is designed to get you certified quickly without sacrificing quality. In just 4 hours, you'll learn life-saving skills and receive your official AHA BLS Provider card the same day.

  • Duration: 4 Hours - Same Day Certification!
  • Certification: American Heart Association BLS Provider
  • Valid For: 2 Years
  • Format: Hands-on skills training with written assessment

What You'll Learn

  • Adult CPR and AED use
  • Child CPR and AED use
  • Infant CPR techniques
  • Choking relief for adults, children, and infants
  • Rescue breathing techniques
  • Team-based resuscitation skills
  • How to use an Automated External Defibrillator (AED)

Who Should Take This Course?

Our CPR/BLS certification course is perfect for:

  • Healthcare professionals (nurses, CNAs, medical assistants)
  • Nursing and medical students
  • Teachers and school staff
  • Fitness instructors and personal trainers
  • Childcare providers and daycare workers
  • Parents and caregivers
  • Anyone who needs CPR certification for work
  • Anyone who wants to be prepared for emergencies

Requirements

  • No prerequisites - open to everyone!
  • No prior medical experience needed
  • Must be able to perform physical skills (kneeling, compressions)
  • Valid photo ID
