A Message from Our Founder


Dear aspiring healthcare heroes, my name is Veronica. I am a registered nurse with thirty years of experience, and I am the proud founder of Mentee College. I've had the privilege of serving and learning in the healthcare industry since the '90s, a journey that was both challenging and profoundly rewarding.


In 2003, I founded Mentee College to transform how we prepare the next generation of healthcare professionals. At Mentee College, we don't just train you for a job; we nurture you into becoming compassionate healthcare providers who truly make a difference. Our curriculum is not only comprehensive and practical, but also ensures our graduates are well-prepared to provide the best patient care. We're not just building your career; we're also helping you serve your community in the best way possible.

Together, let's shape tomorrow's healthcare, today. Welcome to Mentee College.

- Veronica

Our Mission: Empowering Students to Master Critical Healthcare Skills

Transforming lives, one healthcare hero at a time.

Success
At Mentee College, we measure our success by the success of our students. Our goal is to ensure each student gains the knowledge, skills, and confidence they need to excel in their healthcare careers.
Courses
We offer a range of courses including EKG Technology, Patient Care Technology, Phlebotomy, and more, each designed to equip students with practical skills for immediate success in the healthcare field.
Student-Centric Approach
We believe in fostering a supportive and nurturing learning environment. Each student at Mentee College receives personalized attention and resources to thrive academically and professionally.
Flexible Learning
Our courses are designed for flexibility, allowing students to learn at their own pace, and balancing their studies with other life commitments.
