Ultrasound Program

Sonography uses sound waves to generate images for assessment and diagnosis of various medical conditions. It is commonly associated with obstetrics and the use of sonographic imaging during pregnancy, but this technology has many other applications in the diagnosis and treatment of medical conditions throughout the body. Sonographers use special equipment to direct high frequency sound waves into areas of the patient's body. Sonographers operate the equipment, which collects reflected echoes and forms an image that may be videotaped, transmitted, or photographed for interpretation and diagnosis by a physician. Sonographers begin by explaining the procedure to the patient, as well as recording the medical history, laboratory results, and other imaging test results pertinent to the examination. The patient is guided onto an imaging table and the sonographer selects appropriate equipment settings. Frequently, the sonographer will direct the patient to several different positions to optimize the images taken in the area of interest.

The Medical Assisting, MA program prepares the graduate to be a multifunctional practitioner, qualified to perform advanced front office skills and the direct patient care clinical aspects of the physician’s office. The program delivers practical experience in a selected healthcare environment. Graduates of this program may find entry-level positions for office Medical Assistants, EKG Technicians, Phlebotomy Technicians, and Unit/Ward clerks in a hospital setting. The focus of this training program is to in class provide the students with the fundamental knowledge needed to perform through reinforced academic and occupational knowledge, skills and attitudes required for a medical assistant.

Performing skills of catheter insertion, dressing changes, electrocardiography, phlebotomy, and pre/postoperative care. The focus of the training program is to provide in class the students with the comprehensive understanding of and skills to be able to perform these essential nursing skills competently and safely in an active healthcare facility.

EKG Technician

The Electrocardiography Technician, EKG program is designed for individuals interested in pursuing an entry level of career in a healthcare facility. The subjects covered are medical terminology as related to cardiovascular technology, cardiac anatomy, physiology and electrophysiology, EKG interpretation and arrhythmia recognition. In conjunction with theory, laboratory practice with successful skill performance is also part of the curriculum; which provides the student(s) with hands on training. The focus of this training program is to in class provide the students with the fundamental knowledge needed to perform an EKG and recognize any abnormalities that reinforce academic and occupational knowledge, skills and attitudes required for an EKG technician.

$150

Medical Billing and Coding

The Medical Billing and Coding Specialist, MBCS program is designed for individuals interested in pursuing professional level positions in the clinics, hospitals, insurance agencies, public health facilities, doctor’s office, nursing homes, as well as being self employed. As a medical coder, you will be working with patients, doctors, nurses and insurance companies to accurately code diagnoses and procedures on patient’s medical records ensuring proper reimbursement The subjects covered are medical terminology as related to basic human anatomy and physiology, diseases, disorders and the coding for accounts receivable and payable. In conjunction with theory, laboratory practice with successful skill performance is also part of the curriculum; which provides the student(s) with hands on training.

Phlebotomy Technician

The Phlebotomy Technician program trains the entry level healthcare student to work in an advanced skill area of medical offices, outpatient laboratories, or hospitals infusion therapy nursing and/or the inpatient laboratory department(s); in utilizing their primary responsibility of collecting blood specimens from patients or clients for the purpose of laboratory analysis. The focus of this training program is to in class provide the students with the technical skills and fundamental knowledge needed to perform venipuncture completely and safely in the clinical lab and reinforce academic and occupational knowledge, skills and attitudes required for a phlebotomist in an active healthcare institution.
