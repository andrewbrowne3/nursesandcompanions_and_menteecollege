Certificate in Medical Assistant FAQ

Program Duration & Schedule

  • Length: 6 months
  • First 6 weeks: 3 days a week 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM
  • After 6 weeks: 1 day a week from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM

Application & Requirements

  • Application Fee: $35.00 (non-refundable)
  • TB skin test (The TB skin test must have been completed within the past 12 months, additional immunizations required please call for more information)
  • Mandatory Background Check
  • Two forms of ID (Photo ID + SS Card)
  • High school/GED diploma

Tuition & Payment

  • Tuition: $16,300
  • $200.00 + $35.00 application fee (nonrefundable) due at registration
  • Payment Plan available upon request

National License Exam

  • Exam Fee: Included in tuition
  • Exam consists of 180 questions
  • You have 3 opportunities to pass the exam within one year
  • Retake fee: $190.00
  • National Exam is taken online at the college
