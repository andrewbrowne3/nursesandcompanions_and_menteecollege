    How to Apply

    Applying for our programs is a straightforward process. Just follow these simple steps:

    1. Select the degree type from the dropdown menu below (Certificate, Associates, or Diploma)

    2. For Certificate programs (Nurse Aide/CNA, Medical Assistant), choose "Certificate" - Application fee is $35

    3. For the Practical Nursing (LPN) program, choose "Diploma" - Application fee is $75

    4. For Medical Assistant Associates, choose "Associates" - Application fee is $75

    5. For CPR & BLS Certification, choose "CPR / BLS" - Registration fee is $85

    6. Fill out the form completely with your details
    7. Pay the application fee at the bottom - your application is submitted once payment is complete!

    Questions? Contact us at 770-931-5020
