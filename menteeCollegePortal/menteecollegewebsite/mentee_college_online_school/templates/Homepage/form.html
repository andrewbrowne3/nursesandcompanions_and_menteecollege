How to Apply

Applying for our programs is a straightforward process. Just follow these simple steps:

  1. Select the degree type from the dropdown menu.
  2. For the LPN program, choose the "Diploma" degree type.
  3. Choose LPN in the "Diploma program" dropdown window
  4. Fill out the form with your details.
  5. Pay the application fee at the bottom of the application and your application is submitted!

If you have any other questions, please contact us at 770-931-5020.
